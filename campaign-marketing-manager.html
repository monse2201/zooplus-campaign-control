The case study

How I'd plan a centrally-driven campaign for zooplus.

At Tobipets, I architected the CRM and landing-page infrastructure for a national brand partnership campaign. This case study applies that same discipline — concept, media plan, tracking — to a zooplus Sales Activation Campaign, one of the five central campaign types this role owns.

Disclaimer This is an independent strategic exercise based on publicly available information and illustrative datasets. It does not use confidential zooplus data.

Why a Sales Activation Campaign?

It's the campaign type closest to my real experience: a time-boxed push with a clear conversion goal, run across multiple channels and markets at once — exactly the shape of the Tobipets × McDonald's campaign I built.

The core problem

A centrally-planned campaign only works if the creative concept, the media plan and the local market execution stay aligned — from the first brief to the post-campaign report. Losing that thread anywhere is where campaigns quietly underperform.
